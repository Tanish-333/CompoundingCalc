Privacy Policy

How CompoundingCalc handles your information.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CompoundingCalc ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit compoundingcalc.vercel.app (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Calculator inputs: The numbers you enter into the compound interest calculator (principal, rate, time period, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser. This data is never transmitted to our servers and is not stored, logged, or retained in any form.

Usage data: We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to collect anonymous, aggregated information about how visitors use the Site — including pages viewed, time spent on the Site, browser type, and approximate geographic location (country/region level). This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

No account or registration: CompoundingCalc does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information to use the calculator.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand Site traffic and usage patterns. These cookies collect anonymous, aggregated data only.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google and other third-party advertising vendors to serve and measure the ads shown on this Site. See section 3 below for details.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features on the Site.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

This Site is monetized through display advertising, including Google AdSense. The following disclosures apply to that advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Third parties may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, device identifiers, or IP addresses, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this Site.
  • We do not control the cookies set by these third parties, and their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Your choices:

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by many other third-party vendors by visiting www.aboutads.info or the NAI opt-out page.
  • Opting out of personalized advertising does not remove ads — it means the ads you see may be less relevant to you.

For details on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites policy.

4. Users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and California

EEA / UK / Switzerland: Where the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive require it, you will be asked for consent before advertising and analytics cookies are set, and you may withdraw or change that consent at any time using the consent controls presented on the Site. Where consent is not given, ads may still be shown to you, but they will be non-personalized.

California residents: Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — which, under California law, can include the sharing of identifiers with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. To exercise any of these rights, or to opt out of personalized advertising, contact us at the address in section 9 or use the opt-out links in section 3.

5. Other Third-Party Services

The Site also uses the following third-party services, which process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — Used to load the Inter typeface. Google may log the font requests made from your browser. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — The Site is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel collects basic server-side logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) as part of standard web hosting operations, and Vercel Analytics records anonymous page-view data. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

6. How We Use Information

We use aggregated, anonymous usage data solely to:

  • Understand which features of the Site are most useful
  • Improve the calculator's accuracy and user experience
  • Monitor Site performance and uptime

We do not sell, rent, or share any personally identifiable information with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

CompoundingCalc is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

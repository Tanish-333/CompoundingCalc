About CompoundingCalc

Our Mission

Why We Built This

Most financial calculators are buried inside bank websites, surrounded by product pitches, or too simplified to be genuinely useful. CompoundingCalc exists to do one thing well: show you exactly how your money grows over time, with full transparency about the mathematics behind the result.

Compound interest is the most powerful force in personal finance, yet most people have never seen it visualized with their own numbers. This tool gives you that visualization in seconds — for free, with no account required, and no data collected.

Whether you are 22 and starting your first investment account, 35 and recalibrating your retirement timeline, or simply curious about whether your savings rate is on track — CompoundingCalc is designed to give you a clear, honest answer.

Accuracy

How the Calculator Works

The calculator uses the standard compound interest formula A = P(1 + r/n)nt for the principal investment, combined with a monthly simulation model for additional contributions. This approach is mathematically exact for any compounding frequency — daily, monthly, quarterly, or annual.

When you select a compounding frequency, the tool converts it to an effective monthly rate using the formula (1 + r/n)^(n/12) − 1, then simulates your portfolio month by month. This correctly reflects the compounding benefit of higher frequencies (e.g., daily vs. annual) in the final result.

Important: All results are mathematical projections based on a constant assumed rate of return. Real-world investment returns fluctuate year to year. Past performance of any asset class does not guarantee future results. Use these projections as a planning framework, not as a guarantee.
